The set-up

One splice. One island. One very big head.

ROAR-E is a corridor-3D platformer in the classic mascot-platformer grammar — built for phones first, with a hand-painted look that moves like the key art. Saturday-morning cartoon, with teeth.

Five worlds

Five worlds. One maniac.

Each world is a place Rook has colonised: a facility grafted onto an ecosystem. Five platforming levels, rides and arenas, then a lieutenant who chose the wrong side.

  • W1

    The Shorewrecks

    Beached coast facility

    Beached cargo ships, cranes, tide pools. Rust-red containers on white sand, teal corporate signage, bright noon light.

    Boss — Scuttle, a coconut-crab foreman with one enormous claw and a clipboard.

  • W2

    The Canopy

    Jungle greenhouse belt

    Kapok trunks, canopy platforms, glowing greenhouse domes. Bioluminescent lab-glow under volumetric god rays.

    Boss — Morosa, the sloth warden on a hanging vine throne.

  • W3

    The Scrablands

    Arid scrap flats

    Termite towers, scrap ramps, dust-devil trails. Golden grass, red earth, heat shimmer, scrap-metal glamour.

    Boss — Gnash. Two hyena heads, one body, zero agreement.

  • W4

    Redactedby order of facility management

    The Reefworks

    Undersea glass complex

    Glass tunnels through reef, turbine halls, kelp. Sodium-orange lamps in teal depths. Footage withheld — World 4 ends in a blackout.

    Boss — Volt, the electric-eel director. Waterproof tie.

  • W5

    The Spire

    Volcanic ridge and Rook's tower

    Obsidian ridges, lava falls, the white spire interior. Magma rim light on sterile lab panels.

    Final — Rook. And the cure. And a choice.

How it plays

Smash. Free. Roar. Repeat.

One stick, two buttons, five verbs. The jump is the product: coyote time, input buffering and ledge forgiveness are tuned so a phone feels like a controller — and Bluetooth controllers work from day one.

  • Run
  • Jump
  • Claw Spin
  • Pounce Slam
  • Slide
  • ROAR — when Kip's meter is full
Free tier

Rampage Runs

Something enormous is behind you and the camera is in front. Run toward the screen. Don't look back. (Look back.)

Ember Rush

Timed arenas. Grab every ember before the clock does. Medal ladders on every one.

Boss fights

Five lieutenants who chose Rook. Comedic, not menacing — until phase two.

Platforming levels

Twenty-five corridor levels, five per world. Cages, crates, hidden branches, and one Cub hidden in every level.

Pride Rides

Animal-back autorunners. Bastion Roll: a pangolin, a hill, and a very bad idea.

Bonus rounds

Side-camera crate-and-cage gauntlets tucked off hidden branches. Old school on purpose.

Full Pride

Mirror Trials

Hard remixes of levels you've already beaten, against the clock. Built for the fifteen percent.

Daily Ember Run

One seeded daily challenge. Exactly one, so it never becomes a chore list.

Dev window

Early pre-alpha capture, September 2026.

Straight from the engine, no polish: greybox floors dressed with the finished cages, checkpoints and the clamper enemy. Rory's animation rig isn't wired up yet, so he T-poses through the whole thing with total confidence. The jump, the bounce cages and the one-time TEACH glyphs are real.

Pre-alpha · World 1 · Sep 2026

Muted, loops, 21 seconds. Everything is still being painted.

The camp is the home screen. No start-screen ritual: boot drops you straight into your camp, a small explorable space that physically grows with every rescue. Pearl's Workshop sells cosmetics — visible item, visible price — and every look previews live on your Rory.

Specimen files

Meet the specimens. And the management.

Named characters carry adult-friendly attitude. Only the Cubs are allowed to be cute, and they know it.

  • Specimen V

    Rory Leo Vane

    The hero · half lion · all mane

    Wildlife geneticist. Sabotaged his boss's illegal splice program and got thrown into the chamber for it. Woke up with an enormous head and a cracked ID badge. Runs, jumps, claw-spins, pounce-slams, slides, and — when the meter is full — roars.

    “Smash. Free. Roar. Repeat.”

  • Not a specimen

    Kip

    The Judge · ordinary possum

    Non-hybrid, non-speaking, deeply unimpressed. Judged Rory worth following anyway. Rides on his mane, twitches an ear whenever a secret is nearby, and doubles as the roar meter.

    “His sidekick? Zero percent impressed.”

  • Specimen I

    Nyra

    The one that got away

    A lioness uplifted by Rook's very first splice, years before Rory. Glimpsed, then feared, then fought beside. She did not wait to be rescued, and she is not about to start.

    “She is cooler than him. And he knows it.”

  • The management

    Dr. Cassius Rook

    The villain · fully human, proud of it

    Rory's ex-partner. Gene-splicer, raven owner, cane enthusiast. The only clean, symmetrical, uncreased thing on the island, and he would like to keep it that way. Speaks mostly through the facility PA.

    “The rest of the island is above your pay grade, Specimen V.”

The Pride

Your camp fills up as you free them. Each one staffs a corner of your home screen.

  • Mira harness-maker and quartermaster, fennec ears, welding goggles
  • Pearl runs the Workshop; four arms, two of them crossed
  • Jett runs the Sprint course and narrates himself
  • Bastion the rolling ride; pangolin, spectacles, knitted scarf
  • Oran the sage, with a staff of lab-pipe and driftwood
  • The Cubs one hidden per level, freed into the den — the only ones allowed to be cute

Our promise to players

Defensible in a one-star review.

Trust is the moat. Every design and commercial decision in ROAR-E has to survive that test — so here is the contract, in writing, before the game ships.

  • No energy systems.
  • No wait timers.
  • No pay-to-win.
  • No loot boxes.
  • No gacha.
  • No interstitial ads.
  • No ads at all for paying players.
  • No difficulty tuned to sell anything.
  • No purchase influencing any leaderboard.
  • No dark-pattern popups.
  • Assist mode is free, unlimited, and unjudged.
Step one · free

Worlds 1 and 2. All of them.

Ten platforming levels, two Rampage Runs, Bastion Roll rides, the first Ember Rush arena, bonus rounds, the camp, the Daily Ember Run and every Cub in Worlds 1–2. Roughly two and a half to three hours of real game.

  • No gates, no timers, no nags.
  • The free tier is the demo. The demo is the marketing.
then
Step two · one-time unlock

The Full Pride.

Worlds 3–5, every remaining ride and arena, Mirror Trials, both endings, the post-game relic hunt. Bought once, yours forever, with a restore-purchases button up front where you can see it.

  • Never discounted to nothing. Price integrity is part of trust.
  • Offered once, in-fiction. Then it lives quietly on the map.

Cosmetics in Pearl's Workshop are direct purchases — visible item, visible price, nothing random — and every season's earnable set is as good as the paid one. In the free tier only, you may be offered a strictly opt-in rewarded ad (a retry token, an ember doubler): capped per day, never an interstitial, never a banner, and gone entirely once you unlock.

The studio

An Australian indie studio with one very loud lion.

ALPHA 444 IP Pty Ltd is an independent games studio based in Australia. ROAR-E is our first title.

It is a corridor-3D platformer built in Godot 4 with a hand-painted look, tuned for phones first and controllers always. We are a small team. We ship when it's good, and we would rather earn a five-star review than trick a one-star into paying.
